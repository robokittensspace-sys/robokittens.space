What we offer

Learning directions

Each program is hands-on, age-adapted, and builds toward real projects and exhibitions.

Robotics & Engineering

Children build and program real robots, learn to solder, work with circuits, and think in systems. Projects progress to autonomous devices presented at exhibitions.

How classes work

Flexible formats

Choose what fits your family — from regular weekly sessions to holiday camps and one-time workshops.

📅

Weekly sessions

Regular classes 1–2× per week, 60–90 min. Consistent progress, a stable group, ongoing projects.

☀️

Holiday camps

Intensive programmes during school holidays. Full days of experiments, building, and exploration.

🎉

Birthday parties

Science-themed or robotics parties — a unique experience that kids actually talk about.

🏫

School visits

We bring workshops to schools and kindergartens. Hands-on STEAM for whole classes.

🏆

Exhibitions & competitions

Kids present their projects publicly — building confidence alongside technical skills.

🎯

Free trial lesson

Come and try before committing. One session, no obligation — see if it's the right fit.
